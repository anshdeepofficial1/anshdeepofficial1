## About me

I'm pursuing **BCA (Hons. with Research) in Data Science at Chandigarh University**. I develop Android applications, responsive websites, desktop software and automation tools, alongside video editing, motion graphics and visual design.

My approach is product-focused: understand the problem, build a reliable solution, refine the experience, and keep improving through testing and feedback. I care about thoughtful interfaces, maintainable code, privacy and practical automation.
